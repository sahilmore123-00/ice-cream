Ingredients Naturals Malai Ice cream – 3-4 scoops Cardamom powder – 1/2 tsp Saffron stands – a few Cornflour slurry – 2 tbsp Rusk biscuits as needed Sugar syrup Pistachios & almonds – to garnish

Recipe:Take a saucepan, add Natural’s Malai Ice cream and once it melts and comes to a boil, add cardamom powder, saffron strands and corn flour slurry and cook till it thickens.
